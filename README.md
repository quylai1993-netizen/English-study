# 🗺️ English in 3 Months – 30 minutes a day

A free English self-study roadmap for beginners. Open the link and start learning. Nothing to install, no account needed.

**Lộ trình tự học tiếng Anh 3 tháng cho người mất gốc.** Mỗi ngày học 30 phút để giao tiếp được hằng ngày, có thêm chủ đề **tài chính – ngân hàng** và **phát triển con người (people development)**. Chỉ cần mở link là học được.

## What you get / Nội dung
- **14 weeks, 87 sessions** (including 3 monthly tests). The plan starts with a 2-week grammar and vocabulary bootcamp, so you can build correct sentences first. Then come 12 topic weeks that mix everyday talk, people development and finance (people development first).
- **32 grammar lessons (A1 → B2)** explained in Vietnamese, with a tense map. There are about **1,300 different words** in total: 10 core words in every lesson, plus a word bank.
- **Spaced review.** The first 4 minutes of every session bring back old words and sentences after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, so you remember them for a long time.
- **A monthly CEFR-style test** checks listening, vocabulary, grammar, sentence building and speaking. You get a score and an estimated level, and see which areas to work on.
- **Hover any English word** to see its Vietnamese meaning. Use **↺ Restart** to start again from the beginning.
- **40 bonus lessons on everyday life and small talk.** Topics: small talk, travel, food, health, home, free time, social life, and knowledge & the world.
- **Each topic lesson takes 30 min:**
  1. Review (spaced repetition)
  2. Warm-up and pronunciation (with IPA)
  3. Key words + 10 core words: flashcards and a quiz
  4. Grammar and sentence pattern: put a sentence back in the right order
  5. Dialogue: role-play, with speech recognition
  6. Speaking task: record yourself
- **A personal plan.** You choose your name, goal, start date, study days and the topics you like. A short level check suggests where to start.
- **Progress tracking.** You get a streak, minutes studied per week, a finish date, an on-track / behind status, can-do checkpoints and a weekly confidence chart.
- **Vietnamese on or off** (VI button) and **dark mode**.

## Your data / Dữ liệu của bạn
Everything is saved **only in your own browser**: your plan, progress and voice recordings. Nothing is sent to a server.
- To move to another device, download a backup with ⬇, then restore it there with ⬆.
- Clearing your browser data deletes your progress.

Mọi dữ liệu chỉ lưu trên trình duyệt của bạn. Muốn đổi máy thì dùng ⬇ để tải bản sao lưu và ⬆ để khôi phục.

## Tips
- Use **Chrome or Edge** for the best English voices and speech recognition.
- The first time you use 🎤 or ⏺, allow the microphone.
- Speech recognition needs an internet connection.

## Host it yourself
`index.html` is a single file with everything inside, so any static host works, for example GitHub Pages, Netlify or a USB stick.

On GitHub Pages:
1. Put `index.html` in a public repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch** and choose `main` and `/ (root)`.
3. Open the link GitHub gives you.
